About Civora

Civora is a concept civic platform built as a Bootstrap project. The idea is simple: every citizen becomes a sensor for their own city — reporting potholes, broken signals, traffic violations, and pollution directly to the authorities who can fix them.

Reports move through a live status pipeline — from Reported, to Authorities Notified, to Resolved — and the reporter only earns a reward once the issue is actually verified fixed, keeping the system honest.

Alongside reporting, Civora also shows live-updating city stats — traffic, air quality, power grid load, and public transit performance — giving residents a single place to see how their city is doing right now.
